Local_X and Local_Y are lateral/longitudinal coordinates of the FRONT CENTER, in feet. Official Peachtree Metadata, attribute definitions, printed page 13 (extracted lines 604–617). Origin: southern study boundary. FRONT = Local_Y; CENTER = Local_Y - sign(direction)*length/2; REAR = Local_Y - sign(direction)*length. NB sign +1; SB -1. Center/rear are longitudinal straight-path projections, not surveyed body coordinates in curves. Length must NOT be added to Local_Y. Space headway is front-to-front, not bumper gap. No stop-bar coordinate is specified by these definitions.
